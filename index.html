// 01 — ABOUT CIPHER
WHO WE ARE

CIPHER is the official IT Students' Executive Council of MIT Academy of Engineering, Pune. We are a community of curious minds, relentless builders, and forward-thinking innovators united by one purpose — to decode the future of technology.

From organizing cutting-edge tech talks and hackathons to fostering industry connections, CIPHER exists to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world innovation.

We don't just study technology. We shape it.
